### Datasets & Baselines

1. **Synthetic Fare Dataset:** 6,720 rows generated by `train.py` (`data/fare_training.csv`) with seed 42 across 8 Indian cities and 6 transit types to train the Ridge regression model.
2. **Synthetic Risk Dataset:** 6,500 scenarios generated in `train.py` (`data/risk_training.csv`) to train the Random Forest risk classifier.
3. **Synthetic Price Anomaly Dataset:** 1,500 price ratio pairs generated in `train.py` (`data/anomaly_training.csv`) to fit the Isolation Forest model.
4. **Synthetic Complaint Text Dataset:** 40 original demonstration sentences (`data/text_training.json`) to train the TF-IDF and Logistic Regression text categorizer.
5. **Educational Tourist Trap Patterns:** Illustrative scenarios built into `data.js` covering common tourist traps and recommended precautions.
