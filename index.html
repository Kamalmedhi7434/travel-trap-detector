01 / FARE MODEL

Learn the fair-price estimate

Ridge regression learns city/service intercepts, quantity rates and ride-night effects from 6,720 synthetic training prices. A held-out error band supports a conservative working range.

With at least 3 recent comparable paid-fare reports, the local median contributes 70% and the model 30%. Records must match city, service and time; quantity must be half to twice the requested amount. Outliers below half or above 1.8 times the median are excluded.

02 / RISK + ANOMALY

Predict caution, not guilt

A random forest predicts Fair, Suspicious or High Risk from the quote-to-estimate ratio, your situation warnings, recent complaints and an anomaly signal.

Isolation Forest learns a cluster of normal synthetic price ratios. Its negative decision score flags an unusual price, not a proven scam. The displayed caution index weights the risk model's class probabilities.

03 / TEXT MODEL

Read report patterns

TF-IDF turns English report words into numbers. Logistic regression predicts hidden charges, payment pressure, diversion or positive experience. Influential words appear beside the report.

The tiny 40-example text dataset is demonstration-only. Its held-out accuracy is 37.5%. Low-confidence or unknown wording is shown as unclear. It does not decide the fare verdict.

What this app does not claim

All four models are real, trained scikit-learn models running locally. Training data is synthetic, not verified market data or real scam incidents. Synthetic test results do not establish real-world accuracy. A caution index is not a probability of fraud. “Fair” is not a safety guarantee.

Traveller reports are unverified and private to your PC's SQLite database. This is not a multi-user community network. The offline diagram does not show real street positions, directions or live nearby providers. Built-in patterns are educational examples, not allegations against areas or businesses.

Ask for an itemized total, compare another quote, and check official information where available. Luxury services, holidays, luggage, waiting and taxes may change the real total. Do not confront someone based only on this app.

Privacy, installation and offline use

No analytics, remote fonts, cloud APIs or account. Optional GPS only chooses a city in browser memory. SQLite stores reports, checks and evidence on your PC. Export preserves reports and evidence; delete removes them from the database.

Keep python app.py running and open http://127.0.0.1:5000. After installing Python dependencies once, ML and database work with Wi-Fi disconnected. App-shell caching is a convenience, not a replacement for the running backend. No phone-to-PC LAN mode is enabled by default.
